📊 Value at Risk (VaR) Dashboard

This dashboard estimates potential losses using Monte Carlo simulation. VaR (Value at Risk) shows the maximum expected loss over a given time horizon at a specific confidence level, while Expected Shortfall (ES) measures the average loss in extreme scenarios beyond the VaR threshold.

Risk Summary

These metrics summarize downside risk. Lower (more negative) values indicate higher potential losses.
